## Horseradish Yogurt Sauce
🍽️ ~200g sauce · ⏱️ 5 min + 15-20 min rest · 🌍 British / condiment · 🏷️ sauce, condiment, to-try, vault

📎 [Vault](vault: 30-knowledge/nutrition/recipes/horseradish-yogurt-sauce.md)

**Ingredients**

- 200g Greek yogurt (full-fat better; 0% works)
- 2-3 tbsp Prepared horseradish (Israel: white chrein)
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp Lemon juice (or white vinegar)
- pinch Salt + cracked pepper
- 1 tsp Honey (optional, to round)
- 1 small clove Garlic, minced (optional)

**Steps**

1. Stir everything together in a bowl.
2. Taste, adjust horseradish/salt/lemon.
3. Rest in fridge 15-20 min -- flavors marry, horseradish blooms.

## Pot Roast (House Version)
🍽️ family (4-6) · ⏱️ ~5 hr · 🌍 American / braise · 🏷️ beef, braise, family-friendly, dinner, vault

📎 [Vault](https://youtu.be/eVuXwv1yxo0)

**Ingredients**

- 3-5 lb Beef chuck roast (chuck only)
- ~1 tbsp per side Salt
- 225g Onions, rough chopped (house swap from shallots)
- 225g Celery, Lego-brick pieces
- 3 chunky Carrots, cut on the oblique
- 4 cloves Garlic, crushed
- per recipe Cooking fat, wine, stock (stock not bouillon)
- 6 large Russet potatoes (for loaded mash)
- 1 stick Butter (for mash)
- 1 dollop Sour cream (for mash)

**Steps**

1. Salt the roast generously on all sides (~20g/side). Rest 20+ min while you prep veg.
2. Prep mirepoix: rough-chop onions, celery, carrots (obliques), crush garlic.
3. Sear the chuck hard on all sides -- color = flavor.
4. Sweat the veg in the same pot until edges catch color.
5. Deglaze with wine, scrape the fond, reduce.
6. Add stock (no bouillon), nestle the roast back in.
7. Braise covered in oven, low (~300F/150C), 4-4.5 hours until shreddable but holding together.
8. Rest 30 min before serving so the meat reabsorbs liquid.
9. Loaded mash: wrap 6 russets each in foil, slow-roast alongside roast ~2 hr until tender; smash with salt, 1 stick butter, a dollop of sour cream.
10. Plate: rip a chunk of beef onto the potatoes, spoon over veg, glaze with sauce.
